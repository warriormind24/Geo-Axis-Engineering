A Zambian technical consultancy

GeoAxis Engineering & Surveying Ltd provides surveying, geomatics, engineering support and geospatial solutions to clients in mining, construction, infrastructure, land development and allied sectors. We combine sound surveying practice with modern geospatial technology to deliver accurate, traceable information for planning, design, construction and monitoring.

To provide accurate, dependable and cost-effective surveying, geomatics and engineering support services that help clients plan and deliver projects with confidence.

How we deliver

Every assignment follows the same five stages, so you know what to expect.

  1. Scope and planningConfirm deliverables, accuracy, coordinate system, access and safety risks.
  2. FieldworkControlled observations by competent teams under task-specific risk assessments.
  3. Processing and validationComputation, modelling and independent checks of results.
  4. DeliverablesPlans, models, data and reports in the agreed formats.
  5. Handover and supportClient review, revisions and secure archiving of records.
